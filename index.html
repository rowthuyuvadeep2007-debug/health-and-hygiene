ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why Health & Hygiene Matters

Health and hygiene are important parts of everyday life. Good hygiene practices help maintain cleanliness and support a healthy lifestyle.

🧼

Personal Hygiene

Maintain cleanliness of your body through healthy daily habits.

🥗

Food Hygiene

Follow safe practices while preparing, cooking and storing food.

🌱

Clean Environment

Keep your home, school and surroundings clean and safe.

🏃

Physical Activity

Stay active through suitable exercise, sports and regular physical movement.

HEALTH & HYGIENE

Build Healthy Habits

Small habits practiced every day can contribute to a healthier lifestyle.

🧼

👐 Hand Hygiene

Wash your hands regularly with soap and clean water, especially before eating and after using the toilet.

🦷

🦷 Oral Hygiene

Brush your teeth regularly and maintain good oral hygiene to keep your teeth and gums healthy.

🥗

🥗 Healthy Food

Choose a variety of nutritious foods and follow safe food preparation practices.

🏃

🏃 Physical Activity

Include suitable physical activity in your daily routine to support overall health.
