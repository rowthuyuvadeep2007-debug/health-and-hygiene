FOOD & HEALTH

How an unhealthy diet can affect you.

Over time, a diet that lacks variety or contains too much salt and added sugar can raise the risk of health problems.

🩸

Nutrient deficiencies

Not getting enough iron and other nutrients can lead to deficiencies, including iron-deficiency anemia.

⚖️

Unhealthy weight & diabetes risk

A pattern of too many sugary drinks and energy-dense foods can contribute to excess weight and increase the risk of type 2 diabetes.

❤️

Heart and blood pressure problems

Eating too much salt and unhealthy fats over time can contribute to high blood pressure and raise the risk of heart disease.

🦷

Tooth decay

Having sugary foods and drinks often can increase the chance of cavities. Brush twice a day and limit sugary snacks.

These are possible risks, not guarantees. Many factors affect health. Choose a varied diet with fruits, vegetables, whole grains, beans and other nutritious foods.
