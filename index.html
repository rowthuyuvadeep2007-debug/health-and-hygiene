Health and hygiene help us stay clean, active and safe at home, school and in our community.

🧼 Learn to wash your hands

Follow these simple steps. The animation shows the movement you should make.

🚰
👐
Soap + Water
1
Wet your hands

Use clean running water.

2
Apply soap

Cover both hands with soap.

3
Rub your palms

Rub palms together well.

4
Clean between fingers

Do not miss the spaces between fingers.

5
Clean nails & thumbs

Rub thumbs and under your nails.

6
Rinse well

Remove all soap with clean water.

7
Dry your hands

Use a clean towel or air dry.
